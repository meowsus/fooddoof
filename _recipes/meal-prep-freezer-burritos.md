*5 burritos · about 1 hour · easy*

Five hand-held lunches with crispy-edged sweet potato, jalapeño chicken
sausage, black beans mashed just sticky enough, and a bright line of
quick-pickled onion. Assemble the whole batch in about an hour, and future-you
eats like a king who microwaves things.

## Ingredients

### Filling

- 3 cups (12 oz / 340g) frozen sweet potato hash browns
- 3 jalapeño chicken sausage links (about 12 oz / 340g), diced small
- 1 can (15 oz) black beans, rinsed and drained well
- 2½ cups (10 oz) frozen fajita peppers and onions
- 1 cup (5 oz) frozen roasted corn
- 1 red onion, divided — half diced, half thinly sliced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1½ tbsp TJ's taco seasoning, divided
- 1 lime, juiced
- ¼ cup lime juice or white vinegar, for quick-pickled onion
- 2 tsp olive oil, divided
- ⅔ cup shredded cheese, optional but it earns its keep

### Assembly

- 5 large (10-inch) flour tortillas
- Foil squares (about 12") and a freezer bag

## Steps

1. Heat the oven to 425°F (220°C). Toss the frozen hash browns with 1 tsp of
   the oil and ¼ tsp salt on a sheet pan in a single layer. Roast 22–26
   minutes, stirring once halfway, until the edges are crispy and the middles
   are tender. Pale, steamed potatoes turn sad in a freezer — hunt for some
   brown.
2. While the potatoes roast, quick-pickle the onion: submerge the thinly
   sliced half in the lime juice or vinegar with ¼ tsp salt, stirring once in
   a while. Fifteen minutes later it goes from harsh to candy-bright.
3. Heat the remaining 1 tsp oil in a big nonstick skillet over medium-high.
   Add the diced sausage and cook 5–6 minutes until it has brown spots;
   stir in the diced onion and garlic and cook 3 minutes more, until the
   onion softens.
4. Add the fajita mix, corn, and 1 tbsp taco seasoning. Cook 5–7 minutes,
   stirring now and then, until the pan goes from wet to sizzling-dry —
   pooled water now means soggy tortillas later.
5. Stir in the beans, the remaining ½ tbsp taco seasoning, a splash of water,
   and the lime juice. Mash about a third of the beans right in the pan and
   simmer 3–4 minutes. It'll look runny for a minute, then suddenly turn
   thick and gluey — that gluey is exactly right; it's what holds the
   burrito together.
6. Gently fold in the hash browns, then let the filling cool 10–15 minutes
   (honest step, not a suggestion — hot filling in a cold freezer makes ice
   crystals and weepy tortillas). When it's warm rather than hot, stir in
   the cheese if using, so it softens into binder.
7. Assemble: warm the tortillas 20–30 seconds in the microwave so they roll
   without cracking. Down the center of each, lay a small line of pickled
   onion, then a generous 1½ cups of filling just below the middle. Fold the
   sides in, roll tight, and set seam-side down. If the tortilla won't close,
   steal filling out rather than forcing it — a burrito that can't seal is a
   taco with identity issues.
8. Wrap each burrito tightly in foil, freeze flat on a sheet pan for an hour
   until solid, then transfer to a labeled freezer bag. Keeps about 2 months,
   best within 6 weeks.

## Tips & Variations

- Reheat from frozen (foil off, don't thaw): Microwave — wrap in a damp paper
  towel, 2 minutes, flip, then 1–2 minutes more until the center is steaming
  hot; rest 1 minute, and if you want crunch, finish in a dry skillet 2
  minutes. Oven — 400°F (200°C), about 30 minutes, flipping once. Air fryer —
  400°F, 15–18 minutes.
- Want it creamier? Swap the black beans for the TJ's refried beans, heated
  with the same seasoning and lime — the freezer will forgive you.
- Make it vegan: skip the sausage and cheese, add a second can of beans
  (kidney beans work great) plus ½ tsp smoked paprika.
- Stretch the batch to 6 burritos by folding in 1½ cups of cooked rice with a
  splash of water at step 5.
- For more heat, crack in some cayenne or chipotle at step 5 — or use a
  hotter sausage with no changes at all.
