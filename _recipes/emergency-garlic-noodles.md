title: Doof's Emergency Garlic Noodles
---

*1–2 hungry people · 15 minutes · stupidly easy*

Big garlic energy for when the pantry is nearly bare and dinner is already
late. Four cloves of garlic, a knob of butter, and absolutely no regrets.

## Ingredients

- 8 oz (225g) dried noodles — spaghetti, ramen, or udon, whatever's around
- 4 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
- 2 tbsp (28g) butter
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp chili flakes, more if you think you're brave
- 2 scallions, thinly sliced
- 1 tsp sesame seeds, optional but fun

## Steps

1. Boil the noodles in well-salted water per the package, but pull them a
   minute early. Before draining, save ½ cup (120ml) of the noodle water.
2. While the noodles boil, melt the butter in a big pan over medium-low heat
   and add the garlic. Cook gently for 2–3 minutes until it smells incredible
   and barely turns golden — burnt garlic turns this into a cautionary tale.
3. Add the chili flakes and swirl for 15 seconds.
4. Add the noodles, soy sauce, and a splash of noodle water. Toss hard for
   about a minute until everything is glossy and the sauce clings.
5. Kill the heat, fold in the scallions, shower in sesame seeds, and eat
   directly from the pan if nobody's watching.

## Tips & Variations

- Protein trick: fry a beaten egg in the pan first, or crisp up some tofu.
- Make it vegan: olive oil works fine instead of butter.
- Throw in a handful of peas or whatever green thing is wilting in the
  crisper drawer.
- Leftovers barely exist, but they keep a day in the fridge and reheat
  happily in a hot pan.
